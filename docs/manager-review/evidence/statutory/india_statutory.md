- ESI: 0.75% EE / 3.25% ER, ceiling Rs21,000 (Rs25,000 disabled), periods Apr-Sep / Oct-Mar (continue to period end once ceiling crossed). Ceiling continues via savings clause; no new notification found. Contribution rounds UP to next rupee (my knowledge, not re-verified).
- Income tax FY26-27 (incometaxindia "Brief on Budget" via search snippet): new regime slabs unchanged 0-4L nil, 4-8 5%, 8-12 10%, 12-16 15%, 16-20 20%, 20-24 25%, >24L 30%; std deduction 75,000; 87A rebate up to Rs60,000 for taxable income <=12L (marginal relief just above, to ~12,70,588 gross-ish); cess 4%. Surcharge (>50L 10%, >1Cr 15%, >2Cr 25%, old >5Cr 37%; new regime capped 25%) with marginal relief, base = total income after deductions (statute, my knowledge). Income-tax Act 2025 in force from 1-Apr-2026 (section renumbering; TDS on salary s.392 in 1961 Act = new section, not verified).
- Labour Codes in force 21-Nov-2025. Wages: excluded allowances above 50% of remuneration are added back (central rules / transition for EPF reportedly to Nov-2026 [UNVERIFIED]). Gratuity: fixed-term eligible after 1 yr; others 5 yrs (1 yr per some Code readings, [UNVERIFIED]); formula 15/26 x last wages x years; cap Rs20L (not re-verified under Code).
- PT (secondary only, states conflict): MH men >10k Rs200, Feb Rs300 (cap 2,500/yr), women exempt <=25k; KA nil <25k, Rs200, Feb Rs300 (start date conflicts); TN half-yearly slabs (21k nil, 135/315/690/1025/1250); KL half-yearly (slabs conflict); TG 15k nil/150/200; AS; WB annual returns; MP annual income.
