## GCC
- Bayzat (UAE/KSA): custom pricing; one review estimates AED 55-95 PEPM incl payroll+WPS (estimate). Claims Mudad, GOSI, Nitaqat, WPS, EOSB (vendor claim). Differentiator: bundled insurance/benefits.
- Jisr (KSA-focused): estimates SAR 10-17 PEPM (conflicting); GOSI eligibility, Mudad WPS files, EOS, Saned.
- ZenHR (multi-country incl Kuwait, Jordan, Iraq, Egypt, UAE, KSA): SAR 25 or ~$8 per user (conflicting). Only one of three tied to Kuwait. Native IQD/USD called a differentiator in Iraq.
- Paylite lists KW, SA, AE, OM, BH, QA, EG, LB, JO coverage; Malachite: Hijri+Gregorian, Arabic/English, fingerprint attendance (vendor claims).
- Qiwa/Mudad: Saudi gov platforms; WPS integration via file/API (not researched in depth).
- Kuwait rules (sources DISAGREE; verify with PIFSS/adviser): PIFSS covers Kuwaitis (some GCC nationals), expats excluded; employer ~11.5%, employee 8% base up to 10.5% (+2.5% add-on on a lower ceiling), ceiling KWD 2,750/mo; 1%/month late penalty; expats get indemnity under Labour Law Art. 51; bank salary payment required (Law 6/2010 Art. 57) / WPS by 5th. Zoho Payroll Kuwait supports PIFSS max wage + pro-rata.
Table stakes GCC: WPS/SIF file export, GOSI/PIFSS, EOSB/gratuity by service years and contract type, Arabic+RTL, Hijri dates, Nitaqat/Saudization (KSA), biometric attendance, 3-decimal currencies (KWD/BHD/OMR). Not found: specific biometric brands (ZKTeco/Suprema), Hijri-vs-EOSB handling.

## Synthesis
Table stakes by region:
- India: PF/ESI/PT/TDS + filings, Form 16, gratuity, bank files.
- GCC: WPS/SIF, GOSI/PIFSS, EOSB, Arabic/RTL, Hijri, 3-decimal currency, biometric.
- Indonesia: PPh21 (+Coretax), BPJS, THR.
- Europe: national filings (DSN/HMRC) - usually local-player moat; avoid.
Differentiation for tenant-configurable multi-country engine:
1. Most vendors are single-region; ZenHR/Paylite are the regional multi-country exceptions, and they are thin on Kuwait/KWD detail. Genuine 3-decimal KWD + PIFSS (ceiling, split rates, pro-rata) + Art.51 indemnity is a gap.
2. Rule-as-data statutory packs (rates/ceilings/slabs editable per tenant, versioned) vs hardcoded country logic; sources show rates disputed and changing, so configurability + effective dating is the selling point.
3. Review weaknesses to exploit: limited customization (Keka, greytHR), leave-accrual config limits, unannounced downtime.
4. Price anchor: free tiers (greytHR <=25, Zoho 5 users) and ~$5-8 PEPM EU/open-source; target ~Rs 50-100 / AED 20-50 PEPM incl payroll.
Gaps: Freshteam, Pocket HRMS, Qandle, Sumhr, PeopleStrong, Spine, Qiwa/Mudad API specifics, UAE WPS/EOSB detail, LatAm/Africa all unresearched.
