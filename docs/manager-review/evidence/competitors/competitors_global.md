## 3. Emerging trends
- Agentic AI that executes HR actions (approve PTO, change salary, start approvals) with confirmation, not just Q&A: Gusto Gus, Rippling AI, Workday agents, Deel agents [S].
- MCP servers / LLM connectors as a distribution channel: Factorial official MCP, HiBob MCP (beta), Lattice MCP, BambooHR Claude connector (Oct 2026) [S].
- Consolidation into unified suites (HR+IT+finance+payroll); buyers want fewer vendors and TCO scrutiny [S: outsail].
- Governance: EU AI Act treats recruiting/performance AI as high-risk, compliance expected Aug 2026; audit trails for agent actions are required [S].
- Skills intelligence/skills-based talent mgmt [S].
- Global-first: EOR + payroll orchestration blurring into HRIS (Deel, Rippling) [S].
- Only 43% of HR pros rate their HR tech effective (SHRM 2025) [S]; <5% of buyers lead with AI in evaluations [S: outsail, single source].

## 4. Unmet pain points (opportunity)
- Pricing opacity and module cost creep (Rippling, HiBob, Culture Amp, Paylocity, Papaya) [S].
- Support quality and payroll delays (Deel, Remote, Oyster, Paycor, Paylocity) [S].
- Rigid customization/workflows at SMB tier (BambooHR) [S].
- US-only payroll limits (Gusto, BambooHR) [S].
- Complex UX/long implementation (enterprise suites, Rippling onboarding) [S/K].
- Partner-based payroll coverage reliability (Papaya, Oyster) [S].
- White-label/multi-tenant HRMS is essentially unserved by mainstream vendors (only reseller/partner programs) [K].
- Audit/explainability for AI actions; data residency options for mid-market [S/K].

## 5. Data quality flags
